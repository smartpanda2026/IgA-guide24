🎯 IIgAN-PT: International IgAN Prediction Tool QxMD Embedded — Post-Biopsy (Adults) ✓ Result Recorded

📱 Step A — Calculate the Risk using the Official QxMD Tool

The International IgAN Prediction Tool (IIgAN-PT) is the KDIGO-preferred, externally validated risk calculator from Barbour SJ et al., JAMA Intern Med. 2019;179(7):942–952, updated for post-biopsy use in Kidney Int. 2022;102(1):160–172. It predicts the risk of a 50% decline in eGFR or progression to kidney failure over up to ~5–7 years.

The post-biopsy (1–2 year) version is embedded below. It has improved calibration (4-year C-statistic ~0.85–0.87) and is the version KDIGO 2025 emphasizes for risk assessment once the diagnostic window has passed.

💡 Note about embedding: The QxMD calculator is served by qxmd.com and rendered inside an iframe. If the frame appears blank, use the Open in new tab button or the mobile deep link qxcalc.app.link/igarisk.
🔒 PHI: Do not enter patient identifiers in the iframe. Only the de-identified clinical variables (age, eGFR, proteinuria, MEST, BP) should be typed into the QxMD form.
📅 Timing note: This is the post-biopsy (1–2 year) tool. If your patient is at the exact moment of biopsy, ask the QxMD calculator directly on the QxMD site — but for the majority of treatment-planning decisions the post-biopsy tool is appropriate.
↗️ Open in new tab 📱 Mobile / short link
⚠️ Clinical Notes:
  • The IIgAN-PT does NOT include the C-score (crescents). Only M, E, S, T are used.
  • It uses SBP and DBP separately — not MAP.
  • Per KDIGO 2025 Practice Point 1.4.2.3: the tool informs shared decision-making, not drug selection.
Source: Barbour SJ, Coppo R, Zhang H, et al. JAMA Intern Med. 2019;179(7):942–952 (derivation). Barbour SJ et al. Kidney Int. 2022;102(1):160–172 (post-biopsy update). KDIGO 2025 Clinical Practice Guideline for IgAN/IgAV, Practice Point 1.3.1.

🌿 Nefecon (Targeted-Release Budesonide, Tarpeyo/Kinpeygo) 16 mg × 9 months · No taper Fixed 9-Month Course

Nefecon is a targeted-release formulation of budesonide that localizes drug release to the distal ileum. A fixed 9-month course is followed by an observational off-drug period — no taper is required.

💊 Administration:
  • 16 mg once daily — morning, at least 1 hour before a meal
  • Capsules swallowed whole — do NOT open/crush

📋 NefIgArd trial eligibility: Biopsy-proven IgAN · eGFR 35–90 · proteinuria ≥1 g/d (or UPCR ≥0.8 g/g) on RASi.

🧪 TESTING — Oral Methylprednisolone Protocols Two Sequential Regimens Reduced-Dose (KDIGO-preferred)

The TESTING trial used two sequential oral methylprednisolone protocols. The full-dose regimen was stopped early after an excess of serious infections. KDIGO 2025 endorses the reduced-dose protocol.

Sources: Lv J et al., JAMA 2022;327:1888-1898 · KDIGO 2025 IgAN/IgAV Guideline.
🔬 STOP-IgAN — eGFR-Stratified Immunosuppression Two Regimens · 36 months — arm

The STOP-IgAN trial randomized patients after a 6-month optimized supportive care run-in to continued supportive care alone vs. supportive care + immunosuppression. Two different regimens were used, stratified by baseline eGFR. Both were given for 3 years (36 months).

📋 Enrollment & run-in context (applies to both arms):
  • 6-month run-in with optimized supportive care (RASi, BP control) before randomization
  • Randomization at week 24 (end of run-in)
  • Enrollment threshold: proteinuria still >0.75 g/day despite optimized supportive care
  • Exclusion criteria: urinary protein excretion >3.5 g/day at randomization; eGFR decline >30% during run-in
📊 Key Outcomes (NEJM 2015 · JASN 2018 · Kidney Int 2020):
  • Neither regimen prevented eGFR decline over 3 years vs. supportive care alone
  • No benefit persisted at up to 10 years of follow-up (Rauen et al., Kidney Int. 2020)
  • Immunosuppression — particularly the combination regimen — caused more severe infections, impaired glucose tolerance, and weight gain
  • One immunosuppression-related death occurred (in the combination arm)
  • Current KDIGO 2025 guidance favors a reduced-dose glucocorticoid approach (as in TESTING) rather than the STOP-IgAN combination regimen
⚠️ Critical distinction: These STOP-IgAN doses are not the regimen for crescentic/RPGN IgAN.
Sources: Rauen T et al., NEJM 2015;373(23):2225-36 · Rauen T et al., JASN 2018;29(1):317-325 · Rauen T et al., Kidney Int. 2020;98(4):1044-1052 · KDIGO 2025 IgAN/IgAV Guideline · Beck LH et al., Am J Kidney Dis. 2023;82(2):121-175 (KDOQI US Commentary).
🧬 Complement- and APRIL/BAFF-Targeted Agents in IgAN Emerging Pipeline FDA-Approved: Iptacopan · Atacicept · Sibeprenlimab

Two mechanistic classes are transforming IgAN therapy: complement inhibitors (blocking the alternative, lectin, or terminal pathway) and APRIL/BAFF-targeted agents (upstream inhibition of pathogenic IgA production — "hit 1").

Sources: Perkovic V et al., NEJM. 2025;392:531-543 · Barratt J et al., NEJM. 2026;395:465-477 · Lafayette R et al., NEJM. 2026;394:647-657 · Mansour N et al., BMC Nephrol. 2026;27:470 · KDIGO 2025 IgAN/IgAV Guideline.
